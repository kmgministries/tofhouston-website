01 — Our church

A church grounded in the Word

Faith for real life. Truth that brings transformation.

Temple of Faith Houston is a church committed to helping people know God, grow through His Word, and live with spiritual authority.

Through preaching, teaching, evangelism, prayer, and ministry, we create space for people and families to encounter Jesus Christ and experience the fruit of a Spirit-filled life.

Our foundation

Everything begins with God’s Word.

Our church exists to make biblical truth clear, lived, and visible in the lives of people, families, and communities.

Meet Stand Ministry.

Stand Ministry serves through Temple of Faith Houston with a focused commitment to restore, equip, empower, and transform. It is one expression of the church’s mission—not a separate church.

The mission

A transformed world, grounded in the Word.

We believe God has empowered us to envision a transformed world through preaching, teaching, and evangelizing from the divine Word of God.

We are sanctioned to express what God has ordained in His Holy Word as truth and the authority to experience the results of a Spirit-filled life through Jesus Christ.
